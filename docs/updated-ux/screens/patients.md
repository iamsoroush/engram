# Patients Screen

## Purpose

Primary long-term clinical memory surface.

Used for:
- browsing patients
- reviewing historical sessions
- continuing incomplete work
- resolving unassigned sessions

---

## Main Sections

### Patients

Primary patient timeline area.

### Unassigned Sessions

Sessions without confirmed patient association.

These should feel operationally normal rather than error-oriented.

---

## Patient Cards

Patient cards include:
- patient identity
- concise AI-generated history summary
- session cards
- status badges
- quick actions

Summaries should remain dense and scannable instead of narrative-heavy.

---

## Session Cards

Session cards include:
- timestamp
- state badge
- short summary
- quick actions

Examples of badges:
- Needs review
- Processing
- Verified
- Unassigned
