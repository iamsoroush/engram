Memara · Therapy Pre-session brief — the signature surface

Walk in knowing the client. One calm screen, read in the doorway in ~60 seconds, built on the existing patient-memory artifact. It opens with a compact arc strip (a mini-timeline of the whole relationship — not just last session), then sections ordered by clinical urgency: safety first, arc position, recurring themes, since-last + a qualitative trajectory (read from memory, no formal test), and today's prompt. Three states shown: the full brief, the no-risk calm variant, and the graceful AI-down fallback. Spec: redesign-therapy.md §5.1.
