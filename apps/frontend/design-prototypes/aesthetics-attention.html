Attention model & patient strip — design proposal (UX epics 1 & 4)

One severity ladder, rendered in place per session (as strip chips + inline confirms) and aggregated into a Close the day sweep. Static mock — see docs/work/ux-epic-close-the-day.md and docs/work/ux-epic-session-layout-diet.md. Chrome copy is bilingual (fa/en + RTL) in the real app; shown in en here.

The one severity ladder

🩹 SafetyAwareness, opt-out. Allergy · contraindication · consent. Always shown first, never a blocker, never counted as a to-do.
⚠ ConfirmA decision — the "N to confirm". Unconfirmed carried dose · verify AI-created patient · patient conflict · unassigned visit. Merges the verify bar + Needs-input.
SuggestedOptional one-tap. Suggested reassignment · partial match. Apply / dismiss.
NoteNothing required. Low-confidence extraction · missing lot. Calm gray fix-at-source — never counted.
💬 MessageA reply (Pro). Patient question awaiting the doctor. Separate cadence, counted separately.
